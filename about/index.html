Robert Bwire

Lead Consultant. An investment strategist and researcher whose work sits where market opportunity meets the institutions that govern it.

Profile

Institutional experience, applied commercially.

Robert previously served as Head of Strategy and Research at the Kenya Investment Authority, the national agency responsible for promoting and facilitating investment in Kenya.

His work spans investment strategy, research, investment promotion, investor facilitation and investment-climate reform. He has worked with regional and development-sector institutions, and undertakes private consultancy assignments through Africa Rising Investments.

That background shapes how the firm works. Time inside a promotion agency teaches something a purely commercial adviser rarely learns: how public institutions actually make decisions, what they can and cannot do, where an investor’s file genuinely gets stuck, and which of a government’s stated intentions have been operationalised. It is the difference between knowing that an incentive exists and knowing who administers it, on what evidence, and how long it takes.

Investment decisions improve when regional ambition is matched by market-specific intelligence.

How we work

Four lenses, applied in order.

We assess the commercial case alongside the institutions, partners and practical requirements that affect market entry.

01

Market

Is the opportunity real?

Demand, competition, cost structure and the commercial logic behind a sector, before capital is committed to it.

02

Institutions

Who has to say yes?

Approvals, incentives, regulators and investment-promotion channels, mapped to the decision actually in front of you.

03

Partners

Who do you need beside you?

Local operators, public counterparts, financiers and associations, with their roles and interests clearly assessed.

04

Execution

What actually gets it built?

Sequencing, land, permits, people and the practical realities that determine whether a plan survives contact with the market.
