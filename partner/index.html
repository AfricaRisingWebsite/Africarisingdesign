Sponsorship & institutional notices.

A defined space for announcements that our readers have a professional reason to see. Placements are subject to editorial approval.

The audience

Who reads this.

Small, specific and professionally motivated. We publish for people making or advising on investment decisions in African markets.

01

Investors and corporates

Foreign investors evaluating African markets, and businesses entering or expanding across East Africa.

02

Institutions and agencies

Governments, investment promotion agencies, regional economic bodies and ministries.

03

Development partners

Development finance institutions, programme implementers and development-sector advisers.

04

Convenors and intermediaries

Conference organisers, embassies, trade missions and business associations.

Formats

What we will carry.

Announcements with a genuine professional purpose. Every placement is labelled as a placement.

Conference & summit notices

Investment conferences, sector summits, roundtables and policy events relevant to African markets.

Tender & EOI announcements

Requests for proposals, expressions of interest and procurement notices from institutional issuers.

Institutional announcements

Agency, ministry and regional-body notices of relevance to investors and advisers.

Investment opportunities

Specific, substantiated opportunities from credible sponsors.

Professional services

Legal, financial, advisory and operational services serving investors in African markets.

Research sponsorship

Support for a named research output, acknowledged transparently and without editorial influence.

Standards

What we will not carry.

The value of this space to a sponsor depends entirely on the standard applied to everyone else in it.

Anything that misstates a return

No guaranteed-return propositions, no unsubstantiated performance claims, no speculative financial instruments.

Anything unlabelled

Every placement is visibly identified as a placement. Sponsored material is never presented as editorial.

Anything that buys editorial

Sponsorship does not influence what we publish, what we conclude, or who we recommend.

Anything outside the remit

Placements must be relevant to investment in African markets. Relevance is judged by us and the decision is final.
