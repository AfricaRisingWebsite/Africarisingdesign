Advisory built around real investment decisions.

Eight services, grouped by the four questions every investment has to answer. Most engagements draw on more than one.

Lens 01 · Market

Establishing whether the opportunity is commercially real.

01

Investment Strategy

Opportunity assessment, investment positioning, strategic planning and decision support grounded in local context.

Who it is for

Investors, corporates and institutions choosing between markets, sectors or entry routes.

What you receive

  • Opportunity assessment and screening
  • Investment positioning and rationale
  • Strategic options and decision support

02

Market Intelligence & Research

Sector research, market analysis, opportunity mapping, policy analysis and clear investment information.

Who it is for

Boards, investment committees and institutions that need evidence before a decision.

What you receive

  • Sector and value-chain research
  • Opportunity mapping
  • Policy and regulatory analysis

Lens 02 · Institutions

Mapping who has to say yes, and how they decide.

04

Investor Facilitation

Support navigating the institutions, procedures, stakeholders and investment-promotion channels that decisions depend on.

Who it is for

Investors who know what they want to do and need the institutional path to doing it.

What you receive

  • Institutional mapping and introductions
  • Procedural and approvals guidance
  • Engagement with promotion agencies

05

Investment Climate Reform

Consultancy for programmes that improve business environments, strengthen investor confidence and support sustainable private-sector growth.

Who it is for

Governments, development partners and reform programmes.

What you receive

  • Business-environment diagnostics
  • Reform programme design support
  • Private-sector development advisory

06

Regional & Policy Advisory

Advice on regional integration, investment policy, investment promotion and economic development.

Who it is for

Regional bodies, ministries, IPAs and development partners.

What you receive

  • Investment policy and promotion advisory
  • Regional integration perspective
  • Institutional strategy support

Lens 03 · Partners

Finding and aligning the counterparts a plan depends on.

07

Strategic Partnerships & Stakeholder Engagement

Identification and alignment of suitable government, private-sector, institutional and development partners.

Who it is for

Organisations whose plans depend on counterparts they do not yet have.

What you receive

  • Partner identification and screening
  • Stakeholder mapping
  • Engagement and alignment support

08

Rapporteur & Conference Services

Professional documentation, analysis and reporting for investment conferences, institutional meetings and stakeholder forums.

Who it is for

Conference secretariats, regional bodies, IPAs and development partners.

What you receive

  • Real-time session capture
  • Thematic synthesis and analysis
  • Communiqué support and final reporting

Lens 04 · Execution

Turning an approved decision into an operating presence.

03

Market Entry & Expansion

Guidance for organisations evaluating, entering or expanding in East African markets.

Who it is for

Companies establishing a presence, scaling regionally or restructuring an existing operation.

What you receive

  • Entry route and structure options
  • Establishment roadmaps
  • Expansion planning across partner states
