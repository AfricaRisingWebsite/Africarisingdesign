Rapporteur & conference services.

A convening is only as valuable as the record it leaves behind. We provide the documentation, synthesis and reporting that turn two days of discussion into something an institution can act on.

The problem

Most conference reports are transcripts.

They record what was said without establishing what it means, what was agreed, or what happens next. They may also arrive too late to inform decisions.

A rapporteur working from inside the subject matter does something different: listens analytically, recognises which interventions matter, connects them across sessions, and produces a record that reads as an argument rather than a log. For investment convenings that means understanding investment promotion, regional integration and reform programmes well enough to know what is significant when it is said.

Scope

What an engagement includes.

Scoped to the event. A one-day roundtable and a three-day regional meeting are not the same assignment.

01

Pre-event preparation

Review of the agenda, background papers and participant list, and agreement on what the final report must be able to answer.

02

Real-time session capture

Structured documentation of each session: positions taken, points of agreement and disagreement, commitments made and questions left open.

03

Thematic synthesis

Cross-session analysis identifying the themes that actually emerged, rather than the ones the agenda anticipated.

04

Daily read-outs

Where the format calls for it, a summary delivered to the chair or plenary at the close of each day.

05

Communiqué and outcome support

Drafting support for communiqués, resolutions or outcome statements, working with the secretariat.

06

Final report

A complete report delivered to an agreed turnaround, structured for the audience that has to use it.
